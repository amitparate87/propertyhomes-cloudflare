05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Can you help me find a luxury property in Pune?

Yes. We help HNI and NRI buyers shortlist and evaluate premium apartments, penthouses and villas based on budget, preferred location and lifestyle needs.

Can an NRI buy property in Pune without travelling?

Virtual presentations and live site walkthroughs can help overseas buyers evaluate options remotely. Documentation, eligibility and tax matters should be confirmed with your legal and tax advisors.

Which Pune locations do you cover?

Our premium-market focus includes Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar, Boat Club Road, Baner, Balewadi, Aundh, Viman Nagar and Kharadi. Suitability depends on your individual requirements.

Do you charge buyers for property advisory?

Any applicable fee or commercial arrangement is discussed transparently and agreed in writing before engagement.

How do you work with builders?

We discuss the project, objectives and scope first. Representation is undertaken only where the required authorisation or mandate is in place.

How can I verify MahaRERA registration?

Ask for the relevant agent or project registration details and verify them on the official MahaRERA website before proceeding.
